The safety warning label that should come printed on every human being, for the [Warning Label for Humans](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85) bounty on make.fun.

## The label

> WARNING: This human runs on caffeine, sarcasm, and spite. Do not shake. May bite if approached before 9 AM.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the one-line warning label to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (the grader's `/output` mount). Nothing else runs. The label is the whole entry.
